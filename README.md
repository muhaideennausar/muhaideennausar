I’m a full-stack developer and designer. I enjoy building web applications that feel simple to use, look clean, and actually respect user privacy. Right now, I'm putting a lot of my energy into **AuraStack**, a digital services brand I'm building from the ground up.

Lately, I've been really fascinated by the practical side of AI—specifically how we can run powerful models locally on everyday computers. I love experimenting with ways to make technology more accessible and completely private.

## What I've Been Building

### STRUCT
Currently, my main focus is **STRUCT**, a product I'm developing under AuraStack. It's an online resume builder that I designed to take the friction out of making a resume. I wanted to build something for students and professionals that is incredibly easy to use, visually clean, and completely respects their data privacy.

### ProjectGPT
A while back, I wanted to see if I could make AI work smoothly without an internet connection. So, I built **ProjectGPT**. It’s a local AI web app that lets you chat with AI completely offline. The whole goal was to prove that AI can be private, accessible, and run locally on regular laptops without sending your data anywhere.

### IntelliBook
Following a similar offline-first approach, I created **IntelliBook**. It’s essentially a local AI assistant for your PDFs. You can upload documents and chat with them to extract information. Because it runs entirely on-device, your documents stay private and secure on your own machine.
